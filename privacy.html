SpeakFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SpeakFlow may collect, use, and protect information when you use our English-learning application.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, information may include account details such as your email address, learning progress, practice answers, app preferences, and information needed to provide the service.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate your account, provide lessons and learning feedback, save progress, improve app functionality, respond to support requests, and manage subscriptions.

3. AI Learning Features

When you use AI-powered features, the text or other inputs you submit may be processed by the AI service provider to generate learning responses. Please avoid submitting passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive personal information in practice activities.

4. Service Providers

SpeakFlow may rely on third-party providers for authentication, database storage, AI responses, subscription management, and app distribution. These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services under their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

5. Payments and Subscriptions

Subscription purchases may be processed through the relevant app store or payment provider. SpeakFlow does not intend to collect your full payment-card details directly through learning activities.

6. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. Data retention and deletion depend on the services and storage systems used by the application.

7. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal information. Contact the SpeakFlow support team using the official support contact provided in the application to make a request.

8. Children’s Privacy

SpeakFlow is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact our support team.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy as the application changes. The latest version and revision date will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the SpeakFlow support team using the official support contact provided in the application.
