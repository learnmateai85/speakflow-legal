SpeakFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

SpeakFlow ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the SpeakFlow English-learning application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use SpeakFlow, we may collect:

  • Account information: Email address and authentication information used to create and manage your account.
  • Learning information: Learning progress, goals, practice answers, lesson activity, scores, and achievements.
  • App preferences: Language, learning preferences, settings, and other choices you make in the app.
  • AI inputs: Text, questions, and practice responses that you submit when using AI-powered features.
  • Subscription information: Subscription status and entitlement information needed to provide premium features.
  • Support communications: Information you provide when contacting our support team.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Provide English lessons, practice activities, and AI-powered feedback.
  • Save and display your learning progress.
  • Personalize learning experiences and recommendations.
  • Manage premium subscriptions and access to paid features.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Maintain, protect, and improve the application.

3. AI Features and Processing

SpeakFlow includes AI-powered learning and coaching features. When you use these features, the text, questions, and other inputs you submit may be sent to third-party AI service providers to generate responses, explanations, corrections, and learning feedback.

SpeakFlow may use OpenAI to generate AI responses and Tavily to support live web searches when relevant to a request. Information necessary to process a request may be shared with these providers.

Please do not submit passwords, payment-card details, or unnecessary sensitive personal information in AI practice activities.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

SpeakFlow uses third-party services to operate its features. Depending on the feature being used, these may include:

  • Supabase: Account authentication and database services.
  • OpenAI: AI-generated learning responses and feedback.
  • Tavily: Web search functionality for relevant AI requests.
  • RevenueCat: Subscription management and entitlement information.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play: App distribution and subscription purchase processing.

These providers process information as necessary to provide their services and are subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Payments and Subscriptions

Premium subscriptions may be purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play, depending on your device and platform. RevenueCat may process subscription status and entitlement information to help manage access to premium features.

Payment transactions are handled by the relevant app store. SpeakFlow does not receive your full payment-card details through its learning activities.

6. Data Storage and Security

Account and learning information may be stored using Supabase and other service providers required to operate SpeakFlow.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain your account, meet applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

You may request deletion of your SpeakFlow account and associated personal information by emailing: hello@speakflow.app

Please send the request from the email address associated with your SpeakFlow account. We may ask for reasonable verification before processing the request.

We aim to process verified deletion requests within 30 days. Some information may need to be retained where required by law, for legitimate security purposes, or for the resolution of outstanding obligations. Information retained for these reasons will be handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

Deleting your SpeakFlow account does not automatically cancel an active subscription. Please manage or cancel your subscription separately through the relevant Apple App Store or Google Play account settings.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.

To make a privacy request, contact us at hello@speakflow.app.

9. Children's Privacy

SpeakFlow is not intended for children under the age where parental consent is required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable legal requirements. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us.

10. International Data Processing

Our third-party service providers may process or store information in countries other than your country of residence. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be applied in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our application, services, or legal requirements change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised date.

12. Contact Us

SpeakFlow Support
