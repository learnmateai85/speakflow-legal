Delete Your SpeakFlow Account

We respect your right to control your personal information. If you no longer wish to use SpeakFlow, you can request deletion of your account and associated personal data.

1. How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion, send an email to our support team using the email address associated with your SpeakFlow account.

Include the following information:

  • Your SpeakFlow registered email address.
  • A clear request to delete your SpeakFlow account.

Request Account Deletion

2. Information That May Be Deleted

Following verification and processing of your request, we will delete your SpeakFlow account and associated personal information that is eligible for deletion. This may include:

  • Account and profile information.
  • Learning progress and practice history.
  • Saved learning preferences and related account data.

3. Information That May Be Retained

Certain information may need to be retained where required by law, for legitimate security purposes, to resolve disputes, or to meet other applicable legal obligations.

4. Processing Time

We aim to process verified account deletion requests within 30 days. We may contact you if additional information is needed to verify your request.

5. Subscription Information

Deleting your SpeakFlow account does not automatically cancel an active subscription.

Please cancel or manage your subscription separately through your Apple App Store or Google Play account settings.
